# Logitech MX Master 3

I have used the MX Master 3 for many years now but the biggest issue is that it's a bit heavy for my liking, but there is no other mouse quite like this one.

I use the metal side scroll wheel a lot to switch spaces in Arc and Zen browser. The infinite metal scroll I also have used so much that I don't think about it when I use it.

I hope they reduce the weight and stop using the rubber coating on future iterations on this mouse.
