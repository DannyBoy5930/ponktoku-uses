In 2023, I decided to purchase an Apple Mac Mini as my first foray into the macOS space and Apple ecosystem at large.

It's been a pleasant experience thus far using the Mac Mini as my daily driver for development in comparison to Windows. I think it's a great option for developers and being able to use unix terminal commands is such a treat.

Even in 2026, it's holding up well and I haven't ran into any bottle necks with my usage.
